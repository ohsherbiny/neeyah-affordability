# Musharaka planning tools

Two browser calculators for a 15-year diminishing-musharaka home purchase.

| Page | What it answers |
|---|---|
| `scenario_explorer.html` | Every month of the 15 years. Payment split, equity build, money left over. |
| `exit_explorer.html` | Selling before month 180. Proceeds, costs, two breakevens. |

## Running it

Open the file. That is all. No install, no server, no build step.

Both pages are self-contained: the model, the styling and the charts are inside the HTML.
The only outside request is Google Fonts, and they fall back cleanly without it. Save a page
to your phone and it still works offline.

## No real figures

**Every default here is a round placeholder, including the income.** Type your own numbers
into the page. They stay in the browser. Nothing is transmitted and nothing is stored.

This repo holds no contracts, no analysis, no income, and no identity documents. Keep it
that way: it is public so that GitHub Pages can serve it.

## A planning tool, not evidence

The contracts govern, not these pages. Where a term is unresolved the pages say so and give
you the switch rather than picking a reading for you.
